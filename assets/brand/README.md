# Tellurion brand assets

Tellurion is an independent project created and owned by Carlo Cancellieri.

The mark represents a working model of Earth: layered spatial data is organised
around a central `T`. Its deliberately compact silhouette is intended to remain
recognisable in repository, favicon, application, and social contexts.

## Files

- `tellurion-mark.svg` is the canonical, scalable product mark.
- `tellurion-mark.png` is the high-resolution transparent social and raster version.

## Palette

- Basalt navy: `#062a4b`
- Aurora teal: `#24c7b5`
- Spatial cyan: `#36c8f4`
- Signal ochre: `#ffb743`

Keep the mark intact, preserve clear space around it, and pair it with the name
“Tellurion” rather than placing text inside the sphere. Do not use the mark to imply
a hosted cloud service, certification, or third-party endorsement.
