Deliberate boundary

What this evidence does not claim.

  • Italy explorer verified at deployment time. The national release has a live demonstrator, not a durability, uptime, throughput, or support commitment.
  • Stable v0.5.0 binaries are available. Use the verified local demo route; the checksummed 0.3.0 archives remain historical evidence, not the primary route.
  • No Tellurion Cloud, SLA or support service. Evaluation and operation are self-hosted.
  • No production assurance. A working demo does not replace security, capacity, licensing or operational review.
  • No hosted timing claim. Shared-service response times are not benchmark evidence.
