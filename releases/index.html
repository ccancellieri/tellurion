What happens when you open a demo?

Only the selected viewer requests its backend. A sleeping free service may take about a minute to start. Opening this version index does not wake every service or trigger a deployment.

Viewer files are retained with checksums. Remote data, external libraries and live services can still become unavailable. Historical pages preserve their original text; they do not certify current backend availability or promise a service-level agreement.

The final v0.5.0 demo set will be added after that release is verified. Existing links remain available.
